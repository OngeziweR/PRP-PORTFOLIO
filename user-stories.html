Tasks

Task 1: Research the common challenges students experience when purchasing textbooks.
Task 2: Summarize the research findings.
Task 3: Define the business idea.
Task 4: Write a draft of the business concept.
Task 5: Record meeting notes and key decisions.
Task 6: Update the Trello board and upload the agreed business idea.

My Reflection

Working on this user story taught me how important it is to have a clear understanding of the project before developing the solution. I learned that researching the idea and finding additional information can help the team understand the problem better and make more informed decisions about the project.

Breaking the user story into smaller tasks also made the work easier to manage. Instead of approaching the business idea as one large piece of work, we were able to divide it into smaller activities that could be completed step by step. This helped us organise our work and work towards completing it within the agreed time.

My personal contribution to this user story was updating the Trello board and uploading the agreed business idea. Through this, I learned the importance of dividing work into subtasks. Trello helped me see how a larger piece of work can be broken down into manageable tasks, making it easier for the team to track progress and complete work on time.

The process of defining the business idea also gave me a clearer understanding of who the project is intended to serve. I developed a better understanding of the students we are catering for and the need our Second-Hand Textbook Swap platform is intended to address.

Overall, this user story helped me understand that successful project development starts with understanding the problem, researching the idea, identifying the target users, and breaking the required work into manageable tasks.
