Product Backlog Overview

The Product Backlog for the Second-Hand Textbook Swap project contains the planned work required to develop the project from the initial business idea through to system development, testing, documentation, and final presentation.

The backlog was managed using Trello and organised according to sprints and project days. The backlog shows the progression of the project from business planning and requirements gathering to design, development, testing, documentation, and finalisation.

Evidence Explanation

The Product Backlog demonstrates how the project work was planned and organised throughout the development process. The backlog contains activities covering business planning, requirements gathering, system design, user interface design, development, testing, documentation, and final presentation preparation.

Organising the work into sprints made it possible for the team to divide the larger project into smaller stages. Each stage focused on a specific area of the project and provided a sequence for completing the required work.

The Trello board also provided a central location where the team could monitor project tasks, identify completed work, and keep track of the project's progress.

The Product Backlog therefore provided the team with a structured view of the work that needed to be completed from project initiation through to final submission.
