Evidence

1. Team Purpose

The objective of the group was to design and develop a platform known as the Second-Hand Textbook Swap. The platform is aimed at CPUT students and is intended to make buying, selling, and exchanging used books easier and more convenient.

The project also aims to lower the costs involved in purchasing learning materials and encourage the sustainable use of textbooks after they have been read.

Vision Statement

The team's vision is to create a reliable, student-centric platform where students can conveniently access affordable textbooks and collaborate in the process of using shared academic resources.

3. Team Values and Agile Principles

The team values respect for the opinions and contributions of all team members. Effective communication, collaboration, transparency, and feedback are also identified as important values.

The team states that it follows Agile principles by delivering a working product, remaining flexible and adapting to changes, and focusing on continuous self-improvement. The team's priority is the interests of its users, who are CPUT students.

4. Communication Plan

The team uses WhatsApp for daily communication and project updates. The team also conducts 15-minute virtual check-ins to review project progress.

GitHub is used for collaboration and version control. Stand-up meetings are used for brief progress reports and coordination, while weekly meetings are scheduled to analyse the progress made during the week.

5. Goals and Principles

The primary goal is to create a prototype of the Second-Hand Textbook Swap platform with essential functionality such as search, filtering, and responsiveness.

The early development phase focused on the basic structure of the website, including the home page, search, filtering capabilities, and deployment through GitHub Pages.

The second phase focuses on improving the platform by adding features such as a wish list, a user guide, and an admin dashboard. The project timeline also includes improvements to search and filtering, testing, and ensuring that the platform works correctly on mobile devices and tablets.

Reflection

This section will contain my personal reflection on the Agile Team Charter and my experience working as part of the project team.

My Reflection

Working on the Second-Hand Textbook Swap project helped me understand the importance of teamwork, flexibility, and collaboration when developing a software project. As the Deployment and Documentation Lead, my main responsibilities were managing the GitHub repository and writing the project documentation. This role helped me understand the importance of keeping project files organised and maintaining documentation throughout the development process. One of the most important things I learned about Agile is that it allows a team to be flexible and adapt as the project progresses. I learned that a project does not have to be perfect from the beginning. Instead, the team can develop the product gradually, make changes when necessary, and improve it over time. I also learned that team members can organise their work flexibly while still contributing towards the overall project goals. Communication was an important part of our teamwork. We communicated regularly through WhatsApp, which allowed us to share updates and stay informed about the project. I found working with this group to be a positive experience because everyone was hands-on and contributed to the project. I did not experience any major challenges while working with my group, and this made the collaboration process easier. If I were to approach a project like this again, I would challenge myself to think bigger when choosing a project idea. Although the Second-Hand Textbook Swap addresses a real need among CPUT students, I would also consider ideas that could benefit a wider population and have the potential to grow and remain useful over several years. This would encourage me to think more strategically about the long-term impact and scalability of a project. Overall, this project helped me appreciate the value of Agile development, effective communication, and individual responsibility within a team. It also encouraged me to think more critically about how project ideas can create value for users, both in the short term and in the future.
